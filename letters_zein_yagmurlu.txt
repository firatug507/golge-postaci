Yağmurlu Çatılar – 10 letters from Zein (English, original)
1. To the girl in the yellow raincoat: I'm the boy who fixed your umbrella last week. I broke it on purpose. Sorry. Coffee?
2. Mom, the roof doesn't leak anymore. I fixed it before I left. Please stop putting buckets out for me.
3. Dear Mr. Pigeon on the chimney, you are evicted. Signed, the family below. P.S. We'll miss you.
4. To whoever plays piano at 3 a.m.: please don't stop. It's the only thing that helps me sleep.
5. Grandpa, I found your old letters in the attic. Grandma kept every single one. She said yes, by the way. Every time.
6. To my neighbor: the cat isn't yours. The cat isn't mine. The cat has two homes and we are both being fooled.
7. Brother, I kept your room exactly the same. The window is still open. Come home when the rain stops.
8. To the bakery: I stole one bread roll in 1998. Here is the money, with interest. It was delicious.
9. Dear future me: if you're reading this, you finally moved out of the rainy town. Was it worth it?
10. To the stranger who sang under my window last night: you were terrible. Come back tomorrow.
